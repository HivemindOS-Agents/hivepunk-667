## What it is

As the pages show, cardkeys is a folder under tools/cardkeys/cardkeys/ that is meant to be published to the Hugging Face dataset drifterpixel/yaml-keys-in-3-dataset-cards. An open bee-scouts issue says that folder holds data.csv with 44 rows (header dataset,source_url,key_order,key,read_status) plus a README.md carrying passing self-test output, and that the published card should name exactly those two files and their row count, with each listed file link fetched once to confirm it resolves. Related pages describe the general pattern the step asks for: a tool that ships --self-test but is run by nothing has an unbacked claim, so tools are registered with a ctest row, a workflow step or a sibling test. No page I read shows check_live.py or self_test.py inside cardkeys itself.
